# Sample Air Quality & Environmental Advisory Checklist
**Non-official demonstration content — not a municipal operating procedure.**

## 1. Sensor & AQI Verification
1. Confirm AQI monitor calibration status, pollutant breakdown (PM2.5, PM10, NO2, O3), and averaging window.
2. Compare local zone readings against official regional environmental agency bulletins.

## 2. Public Health & Operations Recommendations (Human Approval Required)
1. When AQI exceeds 150 (unhealthy band), prepare draft advisories for sensitive groups, schools, and outdoor municipal crews.
2. Review dust-suppression schedules near active construction sites and inspect heavy-traffic corridors contributing to localized emissions.
3. Escalate sustained high-AQI readings to authorized public health and environmental authorities before issuing any public notice.
