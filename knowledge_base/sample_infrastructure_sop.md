# Sample Urban Drainage & Infrastructure Inspection Checklist
**Non-official demonstration content — not a municipal operating procedure.**

## 1. Drainage Capacity & Telemetry Checks
1. Inspect storm-drain intake sensors and pump station status when effective drainage capacity drops below 50%.
2. Prioritize low-lying underpasses, culvert bottlenecks, and historically waterlogged intersections before peak rainfall arrives.

## 2. Field Maintenance Recommendations (Human Approval Required)
1. Dispatch authorized public works crews to clear debris from blocked storm grates and verify backup pump generators.
2. Log sensor faults, silt accumulation, and structural wear in the municipal asset management system.
3. Coordinate drainage inspection findings with traffic operations so flood-prone roads can be reviewed for preventative barriers.
