CITYPULSE AI – Sample Urban Drainage Inspection
 SOP
Document type: Demonstration knowledge-base document
Version: 1.0
Status: Sample content for software testing only
Important notice: This is fictional, non-official demonstration material. It is not a municipal procedure, engineering
standard, emergency instruction, or substitute for guidance from qualified local authorities.
1. Purpose
This sample document describes a simple way to organize drainage inspection notes in a smart-city
demonstration system. It helps a reviewer record observations consistently and identify information that
needs verification.
2. Scope
The checklist covers visible storm-drain inlets, grates, nearby surface water, access conditions, and
observations reported by authorized inspection personnel. It does not authorize field dispatch or
maintenance work.
3. Suggested observation fields

Zone or asset identifier

Observation date and local time

Observation source: direct, reported, sensor, or simulated

Visible condition of grate or inlet

Visible standing water: present, absent, or unknown

Photo or supporting reference, if available

Reviewer name or role, where appropriate
4. Inspection record checklist

Confirm that the record refers to the intended zone or asset.

Check that timestamps and measurement units are present.

Describe visible debris or blockage without assuming its cause.

Record whether the observation is current or historical.

Flag missing, conflicting, or uncertain information for human review.

Do not treat a model score as confirmation of an actual flood or blockage.
5. Data quality and verification
Record the observation time, location or zone identifier, reporting source, and units. Mark unknown values
as unknown rather than estimating them. Distinguish direct observations from reports or simulated sensor
readings.

6. Escalation and human review
If an observation may indicate a safety hazard, record it and refer it to the responsible authorized
municipal team through established official procedures. The CITYPULSE AI prototype must only prepare
recommendations; a qualified human must verify information and decide what action, if any, is appropriate.
7. Example knowledge-base note
Example: “Zone B, inlet near the north-side junction: grate appears partly covered by leaves in a
demonstration photograph. Observation time and exact location require confirmation. No maintenance
action has been authorized.”
8. Document limitations
This sample is intended to test PDF text extraction, chunking, indexing, semantic retrieval, and source
attribution. It contains no verified city-specific data and must not be used for operational decisions.
