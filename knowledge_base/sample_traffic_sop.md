# Sample Traffic Congestion & Corridor Management Checklist
**Non-official demonstration content — not a municipal operating procedure.**

## 1. Signal & Corridor Verification
1. Verify live traffic speed and travel-time feed provenance (real-time API vs simulated telemetry) and timestamp freshness.
2. Confirm whether elevated congestion is driven by recurring peak demand, rainfall-induced slowdowns, roadworks, or a scheduled special event.
3. Cross-check CCTV or Traffic Management Centre incident feeds before advising corridor diversions.

## 2. Mitigation Recommendations (Human Approval Required)
1. Evaluate adaptive traffic signal timing adjustments along impacted arterial corridors.
2. Identify alternate diversion routes using verified road-closure and flood-depth data so vehicles are not routed into waterlogged underpasses.
3. Coordinate with public transit dispatch to communicate corridor delays and priority bus-lane advisories.
4. Keep emergency vehicle corridors clear when congestion overlaps with heavy rainfall or infrastructure incidents.
